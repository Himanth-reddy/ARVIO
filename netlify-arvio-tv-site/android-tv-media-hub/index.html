Step by step

Get your first source working.

  1. Install the official app

    Get ARVIO from Google Play, or download the signed release APK from the official GitHub releases page. Choose the APK for installation; an AAB is a store upload bundle.

  2. Choose your profile

    Choose or create a profile. In Settings, select your language and appearance. Use separate profiles if different people use different libraries or preferences.

  3. Connect one source first

    Open Settings and connect your server under Home Server, or your playlist under TV (IPTV). For addons, use a compatible manifest URL from a provider you trust. A catalog addon can show titles without supplying playable streams.

  4. Test a familiar title

    Open a title from your connected library and choose a source you can access. Resolution, codecs, network speed and provider availability affect playback. Test before adding a large collection.

  5. Add watch tracking

    Connect Trakt or Simkl in Settings to use supported lists and watch tracking. Check that you are using the intended account and profile before changing watched history.

Useful answers

Before you get stuck.

Do I need Premium for the APK?

No. The Android and TV app is free and open source. Premium pays for the optional hosted webapp.

Why is there a poster but no playable source?

A catalog supplies metadata, not necessarily video. Connect a home server or compatible source integration and check that your account can access the title.

Where do I get the APK?

Use the official ARVIO GitHub releases page linked here. Google Play is the other official installation option.

Still need help? Ask in the ARVIO Discord with your app version, device and error. Keep passwords and provider URLs private.
