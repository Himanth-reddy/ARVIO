Build your own connected media setup.

ARVIO brings the services and servers you already use into one connected interface. The free app serves Android TV, Google TV and Android mobile, while optional ARVIO Web extends supported features to iPhone, iPad, Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Apple TV and compatible smart-TV browsers.

Software, not content

ARVIO does not host, provide, sell, bundle or redistribute media, channels, playlists or streams. You connect services, servers and files you are authorized to use. Availability, quality and playback depend on those sources and your device.
