Step by step

Get your first source working.

  1. Get your provider’s details

    Have your authorised M3U playlist URL and, if supplied, an XMLTV guide URL ready. Keep URLs containing account credentials private.

  2. Open Settings → TV (IPTV)

    Enter the playlist name and M3U URL, then choose Add playlist. Add the guide URL where supported. The channel guide and video playlist are different resources.

  3. Load channels and guide data

    Open TV and choose a group or channel. Large playlists can take longer to load. If programmes are missing, check the XMLTV source and whether its channel identifiers match the playlist.

  4. Test a channel

    Choose a channel you know works through your provider. Check provider connection limits, network access and stream format if it fails. Browsers may reject formats that work in the native Android app.

  5. Make it your own

    Favourite your regular channels and hide unwanted groups. Catch-up requires a provider-supported archive; an earlier programme in the guide is not necessarily playable.

Useful answers

Before you get stuck.

Are channels included?

No. You connect your own authorised provider or playlist. Premium covers hosting, not a TV subscription.

Why is there no programme information?

The guide may be missing, out of date or use different channel identifiers. Check the XMLTV address and matching before replacing a working playlist.

Can I watch in a browser?

Supported streams can play in ARVIO Web. Provider CORS rules, HTTPS, network access and codecs can prevent playback, so test on your browser before subscribing.

Does this cover every provider type?

These steps cover M3U setup. Other provider types have different requirements, and support can differ between Android and web.
