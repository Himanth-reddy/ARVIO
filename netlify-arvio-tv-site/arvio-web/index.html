Step by step

Get your first source working.

  1. Choose your hosting

    For managed hosting, open ARVIO Web and sign in to ARVIO Cloud. The one-time three-day trial lets you check your setup without payment details. For self-hosting, follow the official instructions below using your own API credentials.

  2. Use the intended profile

    On the hosted service, use the same Cloud account and intended profile as your existing ARVIO setup. In self-hosted mode, profiles and settings stay in that browser; local storage is not ARVIO Cloud sync.

  3. Connect a source you can test

    In Settings, connect a supported home server, addon or IPTV playlist. Start with a known accessible item. ARVIO does not include a movie library, channels or third-party subscriptions.

  4. Test on your actual device

    MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio is a useful starting point. HEVC, remux files, lossless audio and live streams can behave differently across browsers. Try your actual sources before subscribing.

  5. Choose how to continue

    Maintain your own installation for free, or join Premium for managed hosting. The free Android APK remains available either way. External player handoff and permitted downloads depend on your source and device.

Useful answers

Before you get stuck.

Is ARVIO Web only available with Premium?

No. You can self-host the open-source webapp without a subscription using your own API credentials. Premium is the optional ARVIO-hosted service.

Does self-hosting include ARVIO Cloud sync?

No. Self-hosted profiles and settings live in the browser. Trakt or Simkl can sync their own supported data, but not your entire ARVIO setup. Clearing site data removes local profiles.

Will everything play on iPhone or iPad?

No. Playback depends on the codecs, container, provider and browser support. Test your sources on your actual device during the trial. Desktop success does not guarantee iPad playback.

What does Premium pay for?

Managed webapp hosting and continued ARVIO development. It does not include movies, channels, provider subscriptions or a promise that every source will play.
